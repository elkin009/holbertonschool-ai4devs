# Team Ideation

## Brainstormed Ideas
1. AI Habit Tracker - Track daily habits and get AI-powered suggestions for improvement
2. Document Summarizer - Upload documents and get instant AI summaries
3. AI Decision Helper ✅ (chosen) - Help users make decisions by generating structured pros and cons

## Problem Statement
People struggle with decision fatigue in both personal and professional life. Making important decisions without a structured framework leads to stress and poor outcomes. AI can provide objective, structured pros and cons analysis to help users think clearly.

## MVP Features
- Input decision text and context
- AI generates structured pros and cons list
- User can add custom pros and cons manually
- User votes for preferred option and saves the decision
- History of past decisions with outcomes
